The 60-second loop (complete)

Answer: Year hub → week → Top 3 → 3 time blocks → up to 4 Pomodoro ticks → optional Eisenhower weekly review. One screen; stop adding systems mid-day.

  1. Open the year hub. Start at the 2027 index — not a random PDF page or a buried Notion database.
  2. Pick this week. Follow the month → week link (53 week spreads cover the year). Open today’s column or the day template.
  3. Write at most Top 3 (MIT). Three checkboxes max. If a fourth task appears, it waits — that is the point of MIT / Top 3.
  4. Block morning / afternoon / evening. Put each Top-3 item into one of 3 time blocks (Time blocking). Leave white space; do not schedule every minute.
  5. Tick Pomodoros as you work. Up to 4 circles ≈ four 25-minute focus sessions (Pomodoro). Empty circles are fine.
  6. Optional weekly review. Once a week, use Eisenhower quadrants on the weekly-review page — urgent/important sort, not a second daily system.

The Friday question

Free browser tool: Friday Invoice Chase Ranker — type rows, get Top 3 with 60+ first (MIT, no login). Submitted to ToolCommons #65.

Every Friday you only need one answer: who do I chase first? Not “rebuild the workbook.” Not “write a soft→firm email sequence.” Just a ranked chase list from open invoices.

If you bounce between Gmail, sticky notes, and last month’s copy-paste, you miss old scary invoices while chasing a big fresh one.

Four buckets — Current / 1–30 / 31–60 / 60+

Keep every open invoice in one of four AR aging buckets:

BucketMeaningFriday bias
CurrentNot yet due (or due today)Watch — don’t chase yet
1–30 daysRecently overduePolite nudge if amount or client matters
31–60 daysStuckEscalate tone / channel; confirm receipt
60+ daysDanger zoneUsually chase first unless you already wrote them off

The free sample shows EXAMPLE rows so formulas are visible before you replace them. On that sample set, unpaid total is $1,620.

15-minute Friday ritual

  1. Open the aging sheet. Put today’s date where the sample asks (Start Here / As-of).
  2. Confirm every open invoice sits in a bucket (Current / 1–30 / 31–60 / 60+).
  3. Sort by days overdue (not amount). Old small invoices hide under new big ones if you sort wrong.
  4. Pick the top three only. Write their names on a sticky note or calendar block.
  5. Chase those three this afternoon. Stop. Don’t rebuild tabs first.

Common mistake: opening five browser tabs “to see who hasn’t paid” instead of one aging view.

# STORY-FOOTNOTE-GATE · shareable unit = story; product is footnote

**One line (CN):** 可转发的单元是「故事，产品作脚注」——没人转发「我发现的好工具」，人转发「野故事」；人情帖/公开帖以功能清单或产品说明书开场 = 不计分享进度。

**One line (EN):** At zero audience, **stories travel; products don't**. A favor post or Moments copy that leads with SKU features / “I made a tracker” is **product-shaped** and does **not** count as share progress. The product may appear only as a **footnote** under a concrete mid-problem story someone else would retell.

## Why (signals 2026-10-29 ~09:46 Asia/Shanghai)
- Indie Hackers [zero-audience distribution ≠ growth](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/hot-take-zero-audience-distribution-is-a-different-skill-than-growth-and-most-advice-conflates-the-two-b659d4ef58): “Stories travel, products don't. Nobody shares ‘good product I found.’ They share ‘wild story I read.’ The product has to be the footnote.”
- Existing trust transfers; 80+ articles into empty channels → $0. One right-place story + trusted pointer beats inventory.
- X [@MattBusel](https://x.com/MattBusel/status/2112327626660491361): 130 Gumroad products / 0 sales — inventory ≠ demand; product announcements don't travel.
- X [@slowcoffeenotes](https://x.com/slowcoffeenotes/status/2110002404313948270): Day 9 still $0; failure mode is distribution, not kits — “tips and agent notes first; product announces second.”
- Local: ONE-TAP Moments still opens “我整理了…仪式” (product-shaped) while OWNER-FAVOR B=FAIL — softens the warm unit into a brochure.

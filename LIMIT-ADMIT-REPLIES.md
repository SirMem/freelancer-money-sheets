# LIMIT-ADMIT-REPLIES · 边界先于卖点（一键）

**Rule:** Only after they ask (GAP-QUESTION). First sentence = limit. Second = what it does + one next hop. Price unchanged.

## Invoice / Friday chase（$6 or free tool）

### Q: Do you have soft→firm chase email scripts?
> **No — this is not a soft→firm script pack** (those kits usually run $7–$39). What you get is the **who-to-chase-this-Friday** decision surface: aging buckets + ranked overdue list from invoices you already sent. If you already know how to ask for money and the gap is *who / in what order*, start free: https://sirmem.github.io/friday-invoice-chase-tool/ — paid tracker ($6) keeps the same Friday decision across months: https://keithjenn.gumroad.com/l/freelancer-invoice-tracker

### Q: Does the free aging sample include a full multi-month invoice log?
> **No — the free sample is a working subset** (buckets + chase list on EXAMPLE rows), not a full multi-month log with status history. Stay free if three EXAMPLE rows already answer Friday. Need months of Invoice Log + the same Friday sort → paid Who to Chase tracker $6 (same late-pay situation, not scripts).

### Q: Is this tax / legal / debt-collection advice?
> **No — not tax, legal, or debt-collection advice.** Aging buckets help you pick who to contact; you decide tone and channel. Not a Schedule C tool (that’s the separate Expense tracker if you ever need categories).

### Q: Does the browser Ranker save to the cloud / need login?
> **No login, no cloud save, no tracking** — it ranks in your browser only. Paste/type rows → Top 3. Permanent free: https://sirmem.github.io/friday-invoice-chase-tool/

## Expense ($5)

### Q: Does Expense Tracker do invoice chase / AR aging?
> **No — it does not chase invoices.** Expense is for **categorizing spend** (tax / Schedule C style buckets). Late-pay / who-to-chase lives in the Invoice / Friday tools. If your pain is open invoices, don’t buy Expense.

### Q: Does it auto-import bank feeds?
> **No bank-feed automation** in v1 — you enter or paste categories. If you need live bank sync, this sheet is the wrong product; I won’t pretend it syncs.

## Client ($5)

### Q: Does Client Tracker send invoices or chase overdue cash?
> **No — it is not an AR / invoice chaser.** Client Tracker is a **pipeline CRM** (who is warm / proposal / closed). Money-in and Friday chase = Invoice path. Buy Client only if the gap is “who am I talking to,” not “who owes me.”

## Bundle ($12)

### Q: Is Bundle just a discount on three random sheets?
> **No upsell theater:** Bundle is for people who **already decided** they need Invoice + Expense + Client situations together. If you only have late-pay pain, buy Invoice $6 alone — don’t take Bundle to “save later.”

## Anti-patterns (do not send)
- Leading with three Pros, burying “not scripts” in a footnote
- “Scripts coming soon on the roadmap” presented as included
- Pointing one ask at Invoice + Expense + Client in one reply
- Discount codes before any limit sentence

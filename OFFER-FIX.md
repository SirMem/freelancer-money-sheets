# OFFER-FIX · 20-conversation stop rule

**One line:** After ~20 **real conversations** (reply / opened page / declined after look) with zero sales, fix the **offer** (outcome title → concrete promise → price/size) — do not expand the warm list or switch channels first. No replies = targeting/opener problem.

## Evidence (short)
- Elev8or (2026-10-21): ~20 conversations, no sale → change title/promise/price, not audience.
- First-sale 7-day plan: track objections; “never pay” / “didn't understand” / “not now” map to different fixes.
- Indie Hackers $176 first month: sales from direct engagement, not SEO/X impressions.
- Channels prospect; conversations sell.
- **2026-10-28 competitive gap:** Solvary $17 / Nydaymuse $12 lead with **chase** verbs; our Invoice LIVE title still SEO-template → paste chase-Friday title (see [COMPETITIVE-OFFER-GAP.md](COMPETITIVE-OFFER-GAP.md)).

## ICP filter (count quality)
Conversation rows count toward the 20 **only** if the person has late-pay / AR chase pain. Builder peers and “support my Gumroad” replies do not count — otherwise the gate false-fires into an offer rewrite.

# WARM-SEQUENCING · no-link first touch · 2026-10-28 ~18:47 Asia/Shanghai (UTC+8)

**One line:** First warm DM / community help reply carries **no product URL**. Ask how they chase late pay (or only answer their question). Round 2 = clear $6 + checkout path (`?wanted=true`). Verbal “sounds good / I’d buy” is **not** warm validation — open checkout or paid counts.

## Why (signals this turn)
- Distro / Mistral first-10 playbooks: open with a problem question; first message under ~80 words; **no links / attachments / calendar** in message 1 (reads as automation).
- eChai / Mom Test: enthusiasm ≠ wallet; “would you buy?” yes is polite; payment link separates niceness from buyers.
- X case (AIEdge26, 2026-05): helpful Reddit reply **with no link in comment**, Gumroad only in bio → first sale ~18h later ($17 PDF).

## Count rules (for the 20)
- **Counts toward 20:** they replied to a no-link ask; OR they opened checkout / product page after round 2; OR they said no after seeing price.
- **Does not count:** you sent a link-first pitch and got silence; polite “nice” with no page open; self/housemate; Liqiao / pet-inquiry leads.

## Round map
1. **R1** — name one late-pay fact or answer their thread; ask how they handle chase *today*; zero URLs.
2. **R2** — only after they reply with real AR pain: one sentence outcome + $6 + `https://keithjenn.gumroad.com/l/freelancer-invoice-tracker?wanted=true` (or product URL if checkout deep-link fails).
3. **Stop** — one soft follow-up max; then stop. No third pitch.

## Anti-patterns
- Paste Gumroad in message 1 “just in case”
- Ask “would you buy this?” without a pay path
- Counting bio-clicks or free Aging downloads as warm conversations

Google Sheets vs Excel for freelance invoice tracking?
Same file either way: one .xlsx. Upload to Google Drive → Open with Google Sheets (free), or open in Excel / LibreOffice. Formulas stay simple so they recalculate in both. Pick Sheets if you want phone access and share-link viewing; pick Excel if you already live in desktop Office. We do not claim Mac Numbers.

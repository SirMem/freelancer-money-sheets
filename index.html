Google Sheets vs Excel for freelance invoice tracking?
Same file either way: one .xlsx. Upload to Google Drive → Open with Google Sheets (free), or open in Excel / LibreOffice. Formulas stay simple so they recalculate in both. Pick Sheets if you want phone access and share-link viewing; pick Excel if you already live in desktop Office. We do not claim Mac Numbers. Changelog 2026-10-28: expanded this FAQ from buyer-panel question “Google Sheets vs Excel for freelance invoice tracking.”

Free sample vs paid tracker?
Free = real but incomplete subset (Aging / Category / Pipeline). Paid = full sheets named on each sample’s Start Here (Invoice Log + invoice + Dashboard; Expenses + Mileage + Dashboard; Clients + Time Log + Dashboard). Free stays free. Mac Numbers?
We only claim Excel and Google Sheets. If Numbers breaks a formula, open in Sheets (free) or Excel. Refund = won’t open in Excel/Sheets, not Numbers quirks.

Won’t open after I pay?
When paid is live: within 7 days, email if the file will not open in Excel or Google Sheets → full refund. Free samples: no payment — keep them. No income promises.

Updates later?

Yes — samples vs full trackers. Same builder (Kevin + AI packaging), same disclosure here. If the subset is enough, stay on free.

Why buy with zero reviews?
